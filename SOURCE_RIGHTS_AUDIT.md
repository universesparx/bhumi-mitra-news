## Confirmed policy distinction

Government of India GIGW templates permit liberal, moderate or conservative departmental copyright policies. Public access is not blanket permission to reproduce or automate collection. A separate NIC/GIGW website has its own reproduction restrictions; these cannot be assumed to apply to Bihar department sites.
